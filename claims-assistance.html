WHAT TO DO IN AN ACCIDENT

4-Step Emergency Protocol

1. Ensure Safety

Turn on hazard lights, move to a safe spot if possible, and call emergency services if anyone is injured.

2. Evidence Photos

Capture clear photos of damage on all vehicles involved, road condition, and nearby traffic signs.

3. Exchange Details

Collect driver license numbers, insurance policy numbers, and contact info of all parties.

4. Contact Advisor

Notify our claims support team immediately to start the paperwork and arrange towing or garage repair.
